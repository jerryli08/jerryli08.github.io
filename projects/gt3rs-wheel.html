GT3 RS Wheel Replica

A replica of the Porsche 911 GT3 RS wheel, made to experiment with vapor smoothing.

A replica of the Porsche 911 GT3 RS front wheel, which I printed in ABS on a Bambu Lab H2D and then vapor smoothed with acetone. The print is 275 mm across, about half scale: my CAD is the full-size wheel, 545 mm across the lips and 264 mm deep (275 / 545 = 0.50), with split spokes running out from a single centre bore to a deep barrel.

Which wheel it is

The GT3 RS runs a 20 in wheel at the front and a 21 in wheel at the rear. My replica is the front one:

Calculation Front or rear?

Front wheel10 J x 20 (20 in rim, 10 in wide), 275/35 ZR 20 tyrePorsche, 911 GT3 RS technical data, 08/2022
Rear wheel13 J x 21 (21 in rim, 13 in wide)Porsche, 911 GT3 RS technical data
Barrel just inside the lips (bead seats)about 507 mm acrossmeasured from the CAD
Depth, face lip to back lip264 mmmeasured from the CAD
  1. A rim's size in inches is the diameter of its bead seats: 20 in = 508 mm, 21 in = 533 mm
  2. CAD: 507 mm, 1 mm from 20 in and 26 mm short of 21 in
  3. Width: 10 in = 254 mm, 13 in = 330 mm. The CAD is 264 mm lip to lip, lips included, so its rim width is under 264 mm: 10 in, not 13 in

Both the diameter and the width match the 20 in front wheel, 10 J x 20.

The J width is measured between the inside faces of the lips, so a real rim is a little wider than its J number lip to lip; rounded.

Calculation How fast the front wheel turns on the car

Front tyre275/35 ZR 20Porsche technical data
Top speed296 km/hPorsche technical data
  1. Sidewall: 35 % of 275 mm = 96.3 mm
  2. Tyre diameter: 508 mm + 2 × 96.3 mm = 700.5 mm, so one turn = π × 0.7005 m = 2.20 m
  3. 296 km/h = 82.2 m/s; 82.2 m/s / 2.20 m = 37.4 turns a second

At top speed the front wheel turns about 37 times a second, about 2,240 rpm. Rolling on its bare lips, the full-size wheel in my CAD covers 1.71 m a turn (π × 545 mm); my half-scale print, 275 mm across, covers about 0.86 m (π × 275 mm).

Nominal tyre size, ignoring tyre growth and squash under load; rounded.
