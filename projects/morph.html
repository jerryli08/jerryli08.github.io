Overview

Morph is a 17-DOF robot that folds itself into more than 150 shapes when you text it what you want. I built the language and control path end to end: messages arrive over iMessage through the Linq API, a local MiniLM-L6-v2 classifier maps them to a shape with GPT-4o-mini as a fallback, and fold sequences are planned with A* and validated in MuJoCo.

The shape library came from all 3^17 joint permutations, filtered with an LLM down to recognizable shapes.
