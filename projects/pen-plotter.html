5-Bar Pen Plotter

Parallel robot with inverse kinematics

A 5-bar parallel robot that draws from Cartesian coordinates, built to learn the linear algebra behind robot arms.

2NEMA 17 steppers
IKCartesian to joint angles

Overview

I built a 5-bar pen plotter to learn how linear algebra and transformation matrices apply in robotics. The 3D-printed arms are driven by two NEMA 17 steppers, each on a TMC2209 driver, programmed in C++ on an Arduino Nano.

You give it Cartesian coordinates and inverse kinematics turns them into target angles for the two motors.
