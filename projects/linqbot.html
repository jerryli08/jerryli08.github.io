LinqBot

Robot arm with three control modes

An SO-101 arm you can run autonomously, drive with your hand through AR glasses, or text.

6.5 hBuild time

Overview

Built at the YC Startup School hackathon at Soma Capital. An SO-101 arm with three control modes: autonomous pick and place of a can, hand tracking from an AR-glasses webcam that drives the arm through inverse kinematics, and control over iMessage.
