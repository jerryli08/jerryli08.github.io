Golden Retriever

Assistive fetch robot

Text it what you need and it drives over, grabs it with a learned grasp, and brings it back.

2ndof 250+ teams, 1,500 hackers
10.5 hBuild time
Photos and video for this project are on the way.

Overview

An assistive fetch robot for people with limited leg mobility. You request an object over iMessage; the robot uses monocular depth estimation and path planning to drive to it, finds it with a side-mounted webcam, and grasps it with an SO-101 arm trained with ACT imitation learning.
